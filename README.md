# Computer Science Portfolio

Welcome to my Computer Science portfolio. This repository contains projects and coursework that I completed while earning my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Southern New Hampshire University.

I created this portfolio to document my growth as a programmer and problem solver. As I continue through the program, each course adds new skills and challenges, allowing me to build on what I've learned while creating projects that demonstrate my progress.

## About Me

My background is a little different from many computer science students. Before pursuing software development, I earned degrees in Psychology and Clinical Mental Health Counseling. Those experiences taught me the importance of communication, organization, and understanding the needs of the people who will ultimately use a product.

As a programmer, I enjoy solving problems by breaking them into smaller, manageable pieces. I believe good software isn't just about writing code—it's about creating solutions that are reliable, easy to understand, and useful to the people who depend on them. I'm always looking for ways to improve my skills and learn new technologies.

## Repository Organization

Each folder in this repository represents a different computer science course. Inside each course folder, you'll find selected projects and assignments that showcase the work completed during that class, along with a README reflection describing what I learned, the challenges I faced, and how my understanding grew throughout the course.

My goal is for this portfolio to serve as both a record of my academic journey and a collection of work that demonstrates my continued growth as a software developer.

Thank you for taking the time to explore my portfolio.
