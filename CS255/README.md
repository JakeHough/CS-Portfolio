# CS 255 Portfolio Reflection

## DriverPass Project Summary

The DriverPass project was about designing a new system for a company that helps students prepare for their driver's license exams. The goal was to create a system where students could study, take practice tests, schedule driving lessons, and track their progress. The system also needed to help instructors and office staff manage lessons and keep driving test information current.

## What I Did Well

I think I did a good job listening to the client's needs and turning them into a complete plan. Each part of the project built on the previous one, and by the end I had a design that supported everyone who would use the system. I also enjoyed organizing the information in a way that was easy to follow.

## What I Would Revise

If I could go back and improve one part of the project, I would spend more time making my diagrams cleaner and easier to read. They included everything they needed, but I think I could organize them better now that I have more experience.

## Understanding the User's Needs

Throughout the project, I tried to think about the system from each person's point of view. Students needed a simple way to learn and schedule lessons, instructors needed to keep track of student progress, and office staff needed tools to manage the day-to-day work. Keeping the people using the system in mind helped make sure every feature had a purpose. A system can have great features, but if it isn't easy to use, people won't enjoy using it.

## My Approach to Designing Software

I like to start by understanding the problem before trying to solve it. Once I know what the client wants, I can organize the information and create a plan before any development begins. This project showed me how important it is to plan first, ask questions, and think about the people who will actually use the finished product.
